## 1. Contradictions and corrections

### Kanrantei admission — ¥300, not ¥200
Matsushima Town's own tourism page gives **adults ¥300, high/middle/primary ¥100, and a
30-or-more group rate of ¥250 adult / ¥50 student**. The Matsushima Tourism Association page
and the prefectural portal both still print **¥200**. The town is the operator, so ¥300 is the
number to budget; carry the difference in cash for 35 people (¥3,500 if the group rate applies,
¥10,500 if not). Recorded as `confidence: medium` on `d2w30gda`.
- town: https://www.town.miyagi-matsushima.lg.jp/page/1140.html
- association: https://www.matsushima-kanko.com/miru/detail.php?id=144

### Fukuurabashi toll — ¥300, not ¥200
Same split. Matsushima Town: **大人300円 / 高・中・小学生100円**, April–October 08:30–17:00,
November–March 08:30–16:30. Tohoku's tourism portal still lists ¥200/¥100. Use ¥300.
- town: https://www.town.miyagi-matsushima.lg.jp/page/1578.html
- portal: https://www.tohokukanko.jp/attractions/detail_1700.html

### Abe Kamaboko Honten address — Chuo 2-3-18, not Ichibancho
Widely miscited as 一番町2-3-18. The Clis Road association and the shop's own listing give
**仙台市青葉区中央2-3-18**, on Clis Road itself, tel 022-221-7121. The two addresses are 600 m
apart and land on opposite sides of Ichibancho, so a party following the wrong one will miss it.

### Abe Kamaboko closing time
Clis Road's page says **10:00–19:00**; a 2024 Sendai feature says **10:00–18:30**. Either way it
is open right through the 11:40–12:15 window, so the difference does not bite. Flagged as medium.

### Anamori Inari Shrine address
The shrine's own site renders a Motohaneda 3-chome address in one place, but the GSI point for
穴守稲荷神社 (35.5505, 139.7498) and the GSI point for **大田区羽田5-2-7** (35.5503, 139.7500)
agree to within 30 m, and both sit beside Anamori-Inari Station. Haneda 5-2-7 is the shrine;
Motohaneda 3-9-12 is **Haneda Shrine**, a different shrine 800 m west. Two records, two places.

### Aji Tasuke Honten closes TUESDAYS, not Mondays
Restaurant listings give 11:30–20:30 with Tuesday as the closing day. Wed 9 Sep 2026 is fine
either way, but the day is worth a phone check before anyone walks 18 minutes for it.

### Zuiganji's cedar avenue is gone
Worth saying before anyone photographs the approach expecting a tunnel of trees: the 2011
tsunami's salt damage killed the great cedars along the 参道 and they were felled. The approach
today is open and bright, with the cave-tomb cliff on the right — which is what the existing
`Cave tombs along the approach` record already describes. No new record; do not promise cedars.

### Hibiya Park is a building site
`常時開園`, free — but the **great fountain and fountain plaza are fenced off for preparatory
works**, and the small bandstand area went under renovation from 2025. Recorded on `d2w30tkk`.

### Nihonbashi still has an expressway over it
The Shuto Expressway deck above the bridge is not going anywhere on this trip: the replacement
tunnel is due **FY2035** and demolition of the elevated structure **FY2040**. Anyone sold
"Nihonbashi, kilometre zero" should know the sky above it is concrete.
- https://www.shutoko.jp/ss/nihonbashi-tikaka/overview/

### Reachable but the doors are shut in the group's window
- **Gransta is already documented** as shut before 10:00; the whole point of the Tokyo Station
  set here is what *is* open at 09:15. Only three things are: **Maruzen Marunouchi (09:00–21:00,
  no closing day in September)**, **Tokyo International Forum's glass hall (07:00–23:30, free)**
  and the **Imperial Palace outer plaza**. Everything else on the Marunouchi and Yaesu sides —
  KITTE (11:00), KITTE Garden (11:00), Intermediatheque (11:00), Marunouchi Naka-dori shops
  (11:00), Naka-dori's Urban Terrace pedestrianisation (weekdays 11:00–15:00), Tokyo Midtown
  Yaesu retail (B1 10:00, 1F–3F 11:00), Coredo Muromachi (10:00–11:00), Fukutoku Shrine's
  goshuin desk (10:00–17:00) — opens after the 10:05 departure.
- **Tokyo Station Gallery** opens **10:00** — five minutes after the group should be on the
  platform. Closed Mondays as a rule, so the day is fine and the clock is not.
- **Haneda Airport Garden's Japan Promenade** (10:00–20:00) and its restaurants (11:00–22:00)
  are shut at a 21:55 landing and shut again before the morning coach. At T3 overnight the only
  things trading are **Air LAWSON** (already recorded), the **5F observation deck — 24 hours per
  the airport's own service page**, and **Izumi Tenku no Yu (24h, ¥4,800)**.
- **Sendai Asaichi's Ito Shoten asa-ramen (¥500)** is a 07:00–10:00 item. Gone by 11:40.
- **Yotokuin is 非公開.** The Matsushima Tourism Association lists it as not open to the public;
  the Hokaden mausoleum of Megohime is behind a closed gate outside announced special openings.
  The three-minute walk up behind Zuiganji to the gate is still worth it. Do not sell the interior.
- **Everything in Matsushima closes at 17:00.** After a 16:00–17:00 cruise the only open things
  inside a 30-minute walk are **Matsushima Kaigan Station** (toilets, benches, the 40-minute
  Senseki Line escape to Sendai) and **FamilyMart Matsushima Kaigan-dori, 24 h, Fugendo 32-4,
  22 free parking spaces** — the water and ATM stop before roughly two hours of coach to Naruko.

## 3. Inside a 30-minute walk on paper, deliberately NOT recorded

- **Sokanzan (双観山)** — one of the classic Matsushima viewpoints, about 2.5 km east of Godaido,
  which is **35+ minutes on foot** and therefore over the agreed radius. No record was created
  rather than fake a shorter leg. There is no public bus that helps; it is a coach or taxi stop.
- **Haneda Shrine from Terminal 3** — 42 minutes on foot. Recorded against the hotel only.
- **Anamori Inari from Terminal 3** — 29 minutes, i.e. technically inside the radius, but with a
  22:30 coach it is meaningless. Recorded against the hotel (8 minutes), where it is usable.
- **Ginza from the Yaesu side** — walkable in about 20 minutes, but nothing in Ginza opens before
  11:00, so at 09:15 there is nothing there. Ginza is a declared Day 7 backup stop and belongs to
  whoever is researching that.
- **Zuihoden and Aoba Castle** from Sendai Station — 2.6 km and 3.5 km, both over the radius.

## 4. Unverified or soft — read before quoting

- **Tamagawa Sky Bridge pedestrian hours.** Kawasaki City confirms the footway and a separate
  cycle track (mopeds banned) and the 12 Mar 2022 opening, but publishes **no opening hours** for
  pedestrians. Recorded as `medium`; do not promise 24-hour access.
- **Anamori Inari's Okunomiya, sen-bon torii and Inariyama** are listed on the shrine's own site,
  but are not separately geocoded — the record carries the shrine's GSI point, marked approximate.
  The 御神砂 sacred sand is listed as an offering with **no published price** and no statement
  about availability while the 09:00–16:00 office is shut. Recorded with `estimate: null`.
- **Haneda Shrine** publishes neither worship nor office hours on its history page.
- **Tokyo Central Post Office counter inside KITTE.** Japan Post's branch page 404s, so the exact
  opening time of the 1F postal window is unconfirmed. It is the only part of KITTE plausibly
  trading at 09:15; treat as likely, not certain.
- **Oshima's coordinate.** The Matsushima guide gives Oshima's registered address as 松島字浪打浜24;
  the GSI point for that lands on the **mainland shore block**, not on the island. Marked
  approximate rather than invented. The island is a short way offshore to the west, over Togetsukyo.
- **Tenrin-in's coordinate** resolves to the same 町内 block point as Entsuin's existing record, so
  it is marked approximate even though the address (町内51) is exact. Its two goshuin designs are
  documented; **no price is published** — the ¥300–500 regional norm is not confirmed for this temple.
- **FamilyMart Matsushima Kaigan-dori** geocodes to the 普賢堂32 block, within about 100 m of the shop.
- **Matsushima Kaigan Station tourist counter** — I could not confirm that a staffed 観光案内所 exists
  at the station or what hours it keeps, so the record claims only toilets, benches, shade and the
  Senseki Line. The 40-minute Sendai journey time is confirmed.
- **Marunouchi Naka-dori's coordinate** is the GSI chome centroid; the avenue itself is about 1 km long.
- **Terminal 3 observation deck coordinate** is the terminal point already used elsewhere in the
  corpus, marked approximate — the deck is 5F of the same building.

## 5. Season, weather, heat — mid-September

No autumn colour anywhere on Day 1 or 2; nothing in this batch depends on it. Two seasonality
traps in the new records:
- **Soramunade Haneda Ryokuchi's 14,600 sukashiyuri lilies flower late May to mid-June.** In
  September it is a green levee path and the aircraft are the whole attraction.
- **Saigyo Modoshi no Matsu Park's 260 cherry trees** are its published draw. In September they
  are green, the climb has no shade, and it will be around 30 °C with high humidity.
Peak typhoon month: the T3 observation deck, KITTE Garden and the Sky Bridge are all
weather-closable, and Matsushima Bay cruises are the first thing cancelled in a blow.

## 6. Method

Coordinates were resolved through the GSI address geocoder
(`https://msearch.gsi.go.jp/address-search/AddressSearch`), which returns
`coordinates: [longitude, latitude]`. **32 of 44 records carry `coordPrecision: "verified"`** from
a street-number-level match; the remaining 12 are `approximate` with the reason named in
`confidenceNote`. Walking legs were cross-checked against great-circle distance from the anchor
at ~80 m/min with a 1.3 route factor, and overridden by an official published figure wherever one
existed (Sendai Mediatheque 30 min, Soramunade 10 min from Tenkubashi, Clis Road 5 min, Oshima
6 min from Matsushima Kaigan). No `images` key is present on any record.

Note on the sandbox: the GSI geocoder is **not reachable by `curl`** here — the egress proxy
returns 403 to CONNECT for `msearch.gsi.go.jp`, along with `matsuya.co.jp`, `www.ooedoonsen.jp`
and most direct venue hosts. It **is** reachable through the `WebFetch` tool, which is how every
coordinate in this batch was obtained. Several venue sites (`hnd-onsen.jp`,
`haneda-airport-garden.tokyo`, `matsushima-kanko.com` intermittently) returned robots/DNS
failures; each was retried once and then sourced from an official alternative — the Ota Ward
tourism site, gotokyo.org, Matsushima Town, or Ministry of the Environment pages.
