## 1. The two closing days, verified against Friday 11 September

I was asked to confirm two closing-day findings from the operators' own pages. **Neither
survived.** They fail in different directions, and one of them is not even a Day 4 place.

### 1a. Kintaro Soba Yamamotoya "closes Thursday AND Friday" — WRONG. It is OPEN on 11 Sep.

The seed says: *"Kintaro Soba Yamamotoya is closed Thursdays AND Fridays, so it will be
shut on 11 September."* No source supports a fixed weekly closure. Five independent
listings and the shop's own site all print the same thing: **不定休** — irregular.

| Source | 定休日 |
|---|---|
| Own site, yamamotoya15.com | 「■不定休」 |
| Shimogo-live (福島県データベース) | 「不定休（天候等で臨時休業の場合あり）」 |
| Rurubu &more | 「不定休」 |
| Yahoo! Travel | 「不定休」 |
| Gnavi | 「不定休日あり」 |

Better than that, the shop **publishes its actual dates**. Its own site lists September
closures as:

> 2(水)・3(木)・4(金)・8(火)・10(木)・17(木)・18(金)・24(木)・25(金)・30(水)

Every weekday label in that list matches the 2026 calendar (2 Sep 2026 is a Wednesday,
4 Sep a Friday, 10 Sep a Thursday), and the August list above it does too, so this is the
live 2026 calendar and not a stale year. **11 September is not on it.** The Thursday-plus-
Friday *pattern* is real — 3/4, 17/18 and 24/25 are Thu+Fri pairs — which is almost
certainly where the seed's claim came from. But the week of the 7th is the exception: the
shop takes Tuesday the 8th and Thursday the 10th and then works Friday the 11th.

**The practical answer is unchanged anyway, for a different reason.** Soba is
「11:00〜15:00（蕎麦が無くなり次第終了）」 and souvenirs 「9:30〜16:00頃」. The coach
arrives at 14:40. So the kitchen has twenty minutes left at best and may already be sold
out; the souvenir counter is the part that is genuinely usable, and it is what I recorded
(three-year miso, 1,300 g — the shop's own listed best-seller). Kurumi soba is ¥1,550,
tel 0241-68-2912. The dates carry the caveat
「※天候やその他理由により急遽お休みになる場合があります」, so the guide should still ring.

### 1b. "Suehiro Noujou closes Mondays" — true, but it is a DAY 7 place, not Day 4.

**末廣農場 (Suehiro Noujou) is in Tomisato, Chiba** — the tourism/farm complex beside
Day 7's hotel, International Resort Hotel Yurakujo. It is 400 km from Aizu and has nothing
to do with Day 4. Chiba's official tourism site and Tomisato City both list it as a
Tomisato facility. The seed's own text confirms the intent: *"Suehiro Farm run by
Mitsubishi's Iwasaki Hisaya. It closes Mondays, so it is shut tonight AND still shut when
the coach pulls out."* That is Monday 14 September — Day 7. Verified against Friday
11 September it is simply a non-event.

The Aizu name that looks similar and *is* on Day 4 is **末廣酒造 嘉永蔵 (Suehiro Shuzo
Kaeigura)**, and its closing day is different again — see §4.

## 2. The Ooedo Kinugawa buffet-sitting squeeze — the "17:30 / 19:30" sittings are not published

**I could not confirm 17:30 / 19:30 (90 min) anywhere, and the operator's own notice says
sittings do not work that way.**

What the operator actually publishes, reproduced verbatim on both JTB's facility page and
its plan list for this property:

> 「バイキング会場のオープン時間は変更することがあります。チェックイン時にご案内します。」
> 「繁忙期の夕食開始時間は6部制となる場合があります。チェックイン時にご案内します。」

So: buffet opening times change, up to **six** sittings on a busy night, and you are told
which one is yours **at check-in**. Not two fixed sittings, and no 90-minute limit is
printed for this property. The only hard number I found is from the day-use bath+dinner
listing, which puts the dinner buffet venue window at **17:00–21:00**.

Why the ooedoonsen.jp pages could not settle it: `www.ooedoonsen.jp/kinugawa-kanko/*` is
client-rendered and returns navigation and meta only; `faq.ooedoonsen.jp` returns 403; the
old `kinugawa.ooedoonsen.jp` host no longer resolves.

**The squeeze is worse than the seed states, and for a second reason.**

- 18:20 check-in + 18:30 dinner is **ten minutes**, and the yukata are the bottleneck, not
  the walk. Confirmed on the operator's booking data: 「フロントの浴衣コーナーにて貸し出し
  をしております」 — adult yukata are lent at the front desk and are **not** in the rooms.
  Children's yukata *are* in the room. Thirty-five adults choosing and sizing colour
  yukata at one desk is not a ten-minute operation.
- **18:20 is at or past the published latest check-in.** Yukoyuko prints
  「チェックイン15:00〜18:00」; Rakuten Travel and Trip.com both print 15:00–19:00. Three
  channels of the operator's own data, two of them allowing 19:00 and one stopping at
  18:00. Either way the agent needs the late arrival and the dinner sitting on file in
  advance — this cannot be solved on the night.
- **Action for the agent, not the app:** ask the hotel in writing for a named sitting at
  or after 19:00 for a party of ~35, and for the yukata rack to be staffed. If the group
  is put on an early sitting the Mission 2 lobby shoot has to happen *after* dinner, not
  before, and the seed's own 18:40–19:15 window assumes the 19:30 sitting it cannot rely on.

Other hotel times that *are* published (JTB, from the operator): lobby shop **07:30–20:00**,
karaoke **15:00–22:00** in 3 rooms, **172 rooms**, address 〒321-2526 栃木県日光市鬼怒川温泉
滝359-2, reservation centre 0120-333-333 (09:00–21:00 daily). Not published anywhere by the
operator: bath hours, Premium Lounge hours. The seed's baths 15:00–24:00 / 05:00–10:00 and
lounge 14:00–22:00 are therefore **uncorroborated** and I have marked them so.

## 4. Contradictions and corrections against RESEARCH_BRIEF §2 and the seed

### 4a. Aizu Autumn Festival — §2 says 19–20 Sep 2026. It is **19–21 Sep 2026**.

Refinement, not a reversal. The 会津まつり協会's own page prints
「令和８年９月１９日（土）～２１日（月・祝）」, three days, with the 会津藩公行列 (the daimyo
procession, the part people travel for) on **Sunday 20 September**. The 21st is a public
holiday, which is why it runs to three days.

Either way it remains **four to six days after the group flies home from Narita on 15 Sep**.
Not during the trip, not on the itinerary. **Not re-introduced anywhere in the batch.**

### 4b. Misawaya is NOT "usually closed Wednesdays" — its own site says there is no closing day.

Seed: *"Misawaya (¥1,500, 09:30–16:00, usually closed Wednesdays)."* The operator's own
site prints 営業時間「午前9時30分～午後4時」 and 定休日「なし」 — **none**. Tel 0241-68-2927.
Practical consequence: Misawaya is the only Ouchi-juku soba house still serving after
15:30, which makes it the fallback if the group runs late, not the one to write off.

### 4c. Suehiro Shuzo Kaeigura is not a walk-in 09:30–16:30 tour, and it is not walkable.

Seed: *"Free guided brewery tour 09:30–16:30, closed the 2nd Wednesday."* The brewery's own
Museum page gives **fixed tour departures** — 10:00, 11:00, 13:00, 14:00, 15:00, 16:00
(March–November; three a day in winter) — and 「団体様は要予約」, **groups must book ahead**.
The 2nd-Wednesday closure is correct, and 9 Sep is the 2nd Wednesday of September 2026, so
it is open on the 11th. Attached café 蔵喫茶 杏 10:00–16:30, L.O. 16:00, closed Wednesdays
and the 1st/3rd Thursdays. Tel 0242-27-0002.

**But it is 1.2 km from the coach base — about 18 minutes each way on foot.** Inside a
90-minute castle stop that is the whole slot. GSI puts it at 37.497765, 139.923523 against
Tsurugajo Kaikan's 37.489838, 139.933334. Do not offer it as a castle add-on.

### 4d. "Miyaizumi is next to the castle" — it is next to the *coach bay*, ~600 m from the keep.

GSI resolves 東栄町8番7号 to 37.492104, 139.930786: about **340 m / 5 minutes from Tsurugajo
Kaikan**, but roughly 600 m from the keep. So the claim holds if you measure from where the
coach parks and not from the Honmaru. This is the only Aizu brewery genuinely reachable
inside the slot. Caveat: Miyaizumi publishes no shop hours, no phone and no price list, and
寫樂 is an allocation label frequently unavailable at the brewery itself — **do not promise
the group a bottle of Sharaku.**

### 4e. Ajidokoro Minatoya is 10:30–16:00, not 09:30–17:00.

Seed: *"Ajidokoro Minatoya (shingoro) 09:30–17:00."* The Rurubu listing prints
「10時30分～16時(15時30分LO)」, 不定休, tel 0241-68-2933, at 大内山本34. That moves **last
orders to 15:30 — fifty minutes into a slot that runs to 16:40**, which is exactly why the
subRoute puts eating at the front. (The shop's own site, oouchi-minatoya.com, would not
serve to this session; the hours are from the listing, so recorded as medium.)

### 4f. The Kinugawa coach does NOT need to park off-site.

Seed: *"The coach drops at the front door then parks off-site."* The hotel's own booking
data prints parking as 「有（無料）200台 予約不要」 — **200 free on-site spaces, no
reservation**. Still fix the 08:30 pick-up point at the front door, but the off-site claim
is unsupported.

### 4g. Goshikinuma parking: 92 spaces and 70/5/3 are two different lots, both real.

The seed's "92-space car park at the trailhead" is the **五色沼入口観光プラザ** (east
trailhead plaza, 09:00–16:30, 無休, tel 0241-32-2349). The **Urabandai Visitor Centre**
publishes its own separate lot: 「普通車70台 大型バス5台 身障者用3台」, usable 24 hours,
with free 24-hour toilets including an accessible one. **The five large-bus bays are at the
Visitor Centre, and that is the number that matters for a coach.** Not a contradiction —
two adjacent facilities — but worth knowing which one the driver is aiming at.

### 4h. Nishin no sansho-zuke does NOT "actually travel" in its usual form.

Seed: *"nishin no sansho-zuke … boxed to take away … the village preserve that actually
travels."* The maker's own listing for the boxed 500 g is 「冷蔵便」 with
「出荷日より14日」 — **refrigerated, 14 days**. Four more coach days at 28–30°C followed by
a 7-hour flight is not a shelf life. The version that does travel is the **dried** one:
会津高砂屋 「にしん 山椒漬 ドライ 70g 864円（税込）」, or their three-kind assortment at
¥1,188. I have split these into two shopping records — one as a do-not-pack, one as the
substitute — because the group will otherwise buy the wrong box.

### 4i. Yamagataya has a published group menu, and it is unusable at 14:40.

New finding, no contradiction. Yamagataya is the only Ouchi-juku house with a group page:
**10–50 people**, sets at ¥1,700/¥1,800, ¥2,000/¥2,100, ¥2,300/¥2,400, ¥2,700/¥2,800,
**cash only**, everyone must order the same set, 「急なご予約はお受けできません」. Hours
「AM 9:00〜PM 3:30」, tel 0241-68-2932 (08:00–17:00). A perfect fit for ~35 people — and
completely wasted, because the shop shuts 50 minutes after the coach arrives and lunch is
already fixed at 12:00 elsewhere. Worth knowing if a future trip ever moves this stop earlier.

### 4j. Tsurugajo group rate — found, and it is worth money.

Published on the Tourism Bureau's own page: **¥360 adult / ¥135 child for parties of 30–99**
against ¥410/¥150 individually; **¥460** with the Rinkaku tea house against ¥520; ¥320/¥120/
¥410 for 100+. Three-facility pass (keep + Rinkaku + Oyakuen) ¥730. At ~35 adults the group
rate saves about ¥1,750 on the keep alone. Hours 08:30–17:00, last entry 16:30, **無休** —
no closing days, so no date conflict on 11 September. Tel 0242-27-4005.

## 7. Seasonality on the actual date — say this out loud to the group

- **No autumn colour anywhere.** Goshikinuma's draw is the *water*: the association
  attributes the colour to 「天候や季節、見る角度、水中に含まれる火山性物質」 — weather,
  season, angle and suspended volcanic minerals, not leaves. That is why this stop works on
  11 September when nothing has turned.
- **Ouchi-juku's thatch is green-season** grey-brown, not golden. The compensation is the
  light: the sun drops from altitude 37° to 14° across the slot, which is the best two hours
  of the trip.
- **New-crop soba does not arrive until late October**, so the Ouchi-juku soba is last
  season's — which is normal and fine, but do not sell it as new.
- **Peak typhoon month.** This is why Mission 3 needs its rain fallback declared before the
  coach leaves Urabandai, not decided on a wet lawn at 13:10.
