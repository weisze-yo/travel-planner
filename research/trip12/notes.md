## 2. Defects in `trip12_app_seed.json` and `to_app_seed.py`

These are mine, from reading the seed rather than the web. **All four matter to the deploy session.**

### 2a. All 34 `mustSee` records have the wrong thing in `whereToFind`
`to_app_seed.py` writes `'whereToFind': ps['best_time']`, so the field holds a **time**
("Any time after check-in; also 05:30–06:30 next morning") where the app and the validator both
want a **literal standing position**. **All 34 are fixed in this session's batches**, with the time
moved into the record's own prose.

### 2b. All 49 `shopping` records have NO coordinates at all
`to_app_seed.py` builds `base` with `latitude`/`longitude`, then constructs the shopping dict
fresh and carries only `base['x']` — so the geocoding is silently dropped. 0 of 49 have a
`latitude` key. The batches supply coordinates where they could be resolved.

### 2c. The "104 approximate places" is really 66 places + 38 shopping items
Split across two collections, because `to_app_seed.py` routes `cat == 'buy'` items to `shopping`.
Any importer or UI that counts "approximate places" against 185 will be wrong.

### 2d. 20 duplicate venue pairs — the same place stored twice under two ids
`to_app_seed.py` emits `D['spotGroups']` (the 51 night/dawn spots) **without checking them against
the stops' own `items`**, so 20 real venues exist twice with two different content-hashed ids —
once anchored to a stop, once as a night spot. An importer that writes both **creates two pins for
one place**: THE ROOFTOP, Hanazono Shrine, Godzilla Head, Golden Gai, Ashiyu Sky Deck, Haneda Grand
Torii, Gyoza no Terui, Sabako-yu, Matsuya HICity, Totsuna-bashi, Takimi Bridge, Naruko Onsen Shrine,
Mochidokoro Fukase, Fureai Bridge, Tsukuba Chuo Park, the H-II rocket, the Tokyo Metropolitan
Government observatory, Shinjuku Kabuki Hall, the Yurakujo bath complex, and Narita Sakura-no-Yama.

**`seed_duplicates.json` in this bundle lists every pair** with a `keep` id and a `duplicate` id,
plus three pairs that are **legitimately co-located** in one building and should both be kept
(FamilyMart Tsukuba Q't + Q't 3F dining; the hotel-lobby 7-Eleven + Vista Lounge; the Kinugawa
lobby photo spot + Premium Lounge). Recommendation: write the `keep` id and merge the duplicate's
`x.timeWindow` / `x.hoursText` / `x.hotelKm` onto it.

## 5. Environment constraints, for whoever runs the next research session

Tested once each, not fought — per HANDOFF §1.

**Works:** Japan's official GSI geocoder
`https://msearch.gsi.go.jp/address-search/AddressSearch?q=<url-encoded>` — returns
`coordinates: [LONGITUDE, LATITUDE]`, note the order. Ordinary venue, tourism-board and airline
sites. `en.wikipedia.org` **article** pages.

**Blocked:** `commons.wikimedia.org` (cache-only), `api.wikimedia.org` (cache-only),
`en.wikipedia.org/w/api.php` (cache-only), `api.openverse.org` (403), `openverse.org` (403),
`nominatim.openstreetmap.org` (robots), `kegon.jp` (robots on every sub-path),
`zao-fox-village.com/descriptions/*` (robots), `granbellhotel.jp` (blocks automated fetch).
`curl` gets **403 on CONNECT** even for hosts that WebFetch reaches.

**GSI's limits, learned the hard way:** it resolves addresses and well-known POI names but returns
`[]` for small POI names (`足湯スカイデッキ`), and resolves rural 字 addresses only to village level
(Kitashiobara came back ~13 km off). **Three traps that a later session must not "fix":**
`白銀の滝` resolves to **Hokkaido**, `大正ろまん館` resolves to **Ehime**, and Shisui resolves to a
block centroid **1.8 km** off. Kabukicho resolves only to the district centroid.

**The consequence that matters: ZERO images were collected.** Every licensing route is blocked, so
all eight batches omit the `images` key entirely — which the validator accepts, and which is
consistent with HANDOFF design invariant 6. **This is the one part of the brief that was not
delivered**, and it needs a session with reachable Commons. See `RESEARCH_COMPLETE.md` §5.
