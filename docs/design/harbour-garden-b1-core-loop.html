Frames 01–12

Batch one

Decisions in batch one

Eight things, and why

The place card is one card with three eyebrows

Stop, Must, or Booking. Same layout, same slots — only the eyebrow, the ON YOUR PLAN line and the primary button change. Three cards would have drifted apart within a month.

ON YOUR PLAN is the card's best row

It answers the real question — can I fit this in — using the free-time lanes the app already derives. No other travel app can do this, because none of them model free time.

Today's big number changes job

Free time when you have some; where you are when you do not; "Day done" in the evening. Same slot, same size — the screen answers whatever the day is actually asking.

The dissolve tracks the day, and it is readable

Midday it is mostly photo; by evening it is entirely drawing. You can tell how far through the day you are before reading a word — and frame 08 proves the end state still looks deliberate.

The demo city is gone

Frame 02 replaces it with a drawing and three honest ways in. "An empty trip that is yours beats a full one that is not" is the rule, and it settles the app's worst-reviewed moment.

Paste is recommended, not forced

OD-8's real question. The old build jumped you straight into Paste with no way round it. Now it wears a pink border — a recommendation — beside two equals.

Emergency is brown and its numbers are stored

Coordinates biggest and selectable, because the likeliest use is reading them aloud. Nothing on the screen needs the network, including the country's emergency numbers.

Gather disappears when the day has no more meetings

And its slot carries what the day left behind — unwritten photos, an unsplit bill. The screen never shows an empty container for something that cannot happen.
