| Operator page | What it prints for 20–49 people, Main Deck adult |

So the travel agency's **¥1,080 is the genuine agency / advance-reservation group rate** and **¥1,350 is the walk-up group discount** — a flat 10% off the ¥1,500 individual fare for anyone who simply turns up with 20+ people and has not pre-booked through the group desk. Neither number is wrong; they are two different products. For 35 adults the gap is **¥9,450**, and it turns entirely on whether the agent actually made a reservation with the group desk.

**Conditions the agency page attaches, and they matter operationally:**
- Advance reservation required. Group desk **TEL 03-3433-5121 / FAX 03-3431-0529** (no reception hours published).
- **「団体見学時におきましては、クレジットカードによるご精算を承っておりません」 — group settlement is CASH ONLY.** For a 35-pax party at ¥1,080 that is ¥37,800 in notes, and it is not a card transaction. The guide needs to know this before 09:00.
- **Coach parking is free** — 「展望台をご見学になる間、観光バスの駐車料金は無料」 — *provided* it is settled in one transaction at the Operation Center. But also 「駐車場が満車時等、留め置きができないときは、一度バスを回送いただく場合があります」: **if the lot is full the coach may be turned away and told to come back.** Worth telling the driver.
- **Top Deck Tour group booking requires 「11名様以上であること」 AND 「旅行代理店様によるご予約であること」.** So a 35-pax group *could* book it — but the fee page gives Top Deck as ¥3,300 web / ¥3,500 counter adult, i.e. **¥1,800–2,000 extra per head over the ¥1,500 Main Deck**, and the tour is 40–60 min on its own and 90–120 door to door. **It does not fit a 90-minute stop.** Recorded as a no.

**Hours, confirmed at source:** Main Deck **09:00–23:00, last admission 22:30**, no closing day (年中無休). Top Deck Tour 09:00–22:45, last admission 22:15. Address 〒105-0011 東京都港区芝公園4-2-8, general TEL 03-3433-5111, height 333 m.

## 3. Tax-free — §2 CONFIRMED at the ministry, and the threshold with it

**Japan Tourism Agency (観光庁), verbatim:** 「**2026年11月１日の販売からは**、制度改正により、出国時に空港等（税関）で物品を国外に持ち出すことが確認された後に消費税相当額を返金する「**リファンド方式**」に移行します。」

So the refund-at-departure system starts with **sales from 1 November 2026**. On **Monday 14 September 2026 the exemption is still deducted AT THE TILL**, which is what §2 says. Nothing in this session contradicts it.

**Threshold and documents, from the National Tax Agency (No. 6559):**
- **一般物品 (general goods): minimum ¥5,000 tax-excluded, PER STORE PER DAY.**
- **消耗品 (consumables): ¥5,000 minimum, ¥500,000 maximum, per store per day.**
- The shop must take 「旅券等の提示」 — **passport presentation**. **NEW AND USEFUL: Visit Japan Web can substitute for the physical passport.** For 35 people at 220 shops' tills that is a material difference — the same QR the group already used at Haneda arrivals on Day 1 works at a Shisui cashier. (NTA marks the page 令和7年4月1日現在, so treat "Visit Japan Web accepted" as operator-dependent in practice.)
- Purchaser must be a 免税購入対象者 — non-resident on short-stay status.

**Shisui-side confirmation that it really is at the till:** the outlet's own service page lists Information Center, coin lockers, ATMs, delivery, nursing rooms and Wi-Fi — **and no central tax-free counter at all.** That negative is the operator's own confirmation that exemption is granted shop by shop. ## 7. The free JR ticket in the footer — what it can actually mean

The app prints, under Day 7 only: *"Free per person: 1 JR train ticket from your city station back to the hotel station."* Nothing else in the extract explains it, so this is inference and is flagged as such in the batch.

**What is verifiable about "the hotel station":**
- The hotel's own free city shuttle boards at **「京成成田駅東口 コンフォートホテル前」** — Keisei Narita Station's east exit. **JR Narita Station is a short walk from Keisei Narita**, so a JR ticket terminating at JR 成田 plus a walk plus the free shuttle is the only combination that fits the wording.
