Point 1

Map sees everything. Plan changes everything.

Same trip, two jobs. They share the detail sheet — tapping a pin on Map and tapping a row on Plan open the same place card — so nothing has to be learned twice.

OverviewMap
  • Every day's route drawn end to end, so the shape of the trip is visible at a glance
  • Transport legs as lines — blue where there is a booking, green where the tour handles it
  • Must pins layered on top: Buy, Eat, See, Do, filterable
  • Tap anything → the place card, with a way through to its day
  • The only screen that wants signal, which is why "keep this area" lives here
EditorPlan
  • The day as a timeline you can actually edit — times, order, what sits where
  • Add a place, a booking, a note; drag a stop to another time
  • Free time appears between stops automatically and stays a lane, not a row
  • Tap anything → the same place card as Map
  • Works with no signal at all, because everything here is already on the phone

Decisions in these screens

Six things, and why

Map and Plan share one place card

Tap a pin or tap a row and you get the same sheet with the same buttons. Two ways in, one thing to learn.

Map is where "keep this area" belongs

It is the only screen that genuinely wants signal, so the offline download lives where the need is felt rather than buried in settings.

Gather is on Today, not a tab

Second thing you see, after how long you are free. That is the order you need them in, and it keeps the bar at four.

The route is drawn in three colours

Green where the tour moves you, blue dashes where somebody holds a booking, pink dots where you wandered off on your own. No legend needed.

Dark mode is ten values, not a second design

Every colour is a token. Toggle at the top of this page to see it — that is literally the mechanism the app would ship.

Desktop is the same app, wider

Tab bar becomes a left rail, and the extra width buys Plan beside Map. No separate desktop build to keep in step.
