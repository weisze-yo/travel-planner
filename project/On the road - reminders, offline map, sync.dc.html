Three things that decide whether the app is trusted on the road
ONE PLACE FOR ANYTHING URGENT
A single strip above the tab bar, and it is empty nearly all day

Reminders, the offline-map warning and the unsent-changes warning all speak in the same slot, one at a time, ranked: leave now beats outside your saved area beats changes stuck on this phone. Nothing appears there that you cannot act on in one tap. When the strip is empty the screen looks exactly as it does today.

Not nagging comes from three rules: one warning per deadline, no repeat unless the number gets materially worse, and it stops on its own once you are moving towards the place. Dismissing it silences that deadline, not the feature.

The map's tiles are the only part of the app that needs the network. Everything drawn on top — your route, pins, sub route — is data you already have, so outside a saved area the map degrades to that, rather than to nothing.

"Not going back" is an honest option — you may have decided to make your own way. It clears the warning for that deadline and leaves a line in the day.
WHEN IT SPEAKS
Amber when free time left drops under 15 minutes, or when a place you are heading to closes within your travel time.
Red when the time to get back equals the time you have. Once, plus one repeat if you are still not moving five minutes later.
Never for anything that is not a deadline you set: no "you have not opened the app", no packing nags, nothing about tomorrow.
WHAT IT COUNTS WITH
Return-by time and the walking minutes come from the sub route you already set, so the whole thing works with no signal and no paid routing. If the walk was never estimated, the app says about 20 min back and marks it as its own guess rather than pretending to know.

OUTSIDE THE APP
Installed to the home screen, a web app can ask for notification permission — but it cannot be relied on when the phone is asleep in a pocket. So the design assumes the warning is only ever seen when you open the app, and a notification, if it arrives, is a bonus. That is why the strip is loud on the screen rather than trusting a buzz.
