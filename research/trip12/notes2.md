## 1. THE BIG ONE — the 16:00 bay cruise DOES run on 9 September

The worksheet flagged this as "critical". It resolves in the itinerary's favour, with
one caveat.

The operator's own timetable page publishes the Nioumaru course as a **通年
(year-round)** timetable: eight sailings, hourly on the hour **09:00 → 16:00**, each
50 minutes. Two seasonal notes, both winter-only:

> 「冬季は9:00の便が中型船（定員70名程）になる場合があります。」
> 「**冬季は日の入りが早いため16:00の便が休航になります。**」

So the 16:00 is suspended *in winter because sunset is early* — not in September.
Sunset at Matsushima on 9 Sep is 17:54. Independently confirmed by the operator's
own "本日の時刻表" post, which lists 9:00→9:50 through **16:00→16:50**.

**The caveat is the 10 minutes, not the season.** The 16:00 is the **last boat of the
day** and it berths at **16:50**; the coach departs at **17:00**. There is no later
sailing to fall back on and no slack anywhere upstream (13:18 market → 14:20 temple →
15:22 Godaido). Board by 15:50 with the guide holding all 35 tickets.

- Source: https://www.matsushima.or.jp/timesheet/index.html
- Source: https://www.matsushima.or.jp/blog/news/989/

### Group discount the plan does not appear to use
「団体（15名以上）1割引」→ **¥1,350** adult instead of ¥1,500 on a 35-person party
(saves ~¥5,250). Child ¥750. 2F 展望デッキ グリーン supplement **+¥600 adult /
+¥300 child**, not discounted. Web booking carries its own additional discount, and
the operator calls back to confirm group bookings.

### The actual September risk is typhoon, not the timetable
> 「台風などにより安全が確保できない場合は運航中止とさせていただきます。」

Early-to-mid September is peak typhoon month. Cancellation by phone, fax or email
before the previous day's close carries **no fee**. Ticket office 022-354-2233,
8:00–17:00.
**Wet-weather fallback at the pier:** かき松島こうは 松島スカイレストラン店, directly
in front of the 遊覧船乗り場, 11:00–17:00 Tue–Fri, **closed Mondays** — so it is open
on a Wednesday, it seats a group, and it serves oysters year-round (fried set ¥2,200,
smoked platter ¥2,200, fried-and-grilled set ¥2,900).
Source: http://www.kakikouha.com/matsusimasukairesutoran.html

## 2. Worksheet claims that are WRONG and are corrected in the batch

### 2a. The Yakigaki House grills are NOT standing idle in September
The worksheet's place record and mustSee copy both say the upstairs grill hall "does
not really get going until November" and shows "ranks of charcoal grills standing
idle, waiting for the season". The operator says the opposite:

> 「**通年で食べ放題をお楽しみいただけますが**、特に、11月～3月の旬の時期のおいしさは格別です。」

Weekday hours **10:00–15:00**, so at 13:18 the grills are lit and serving. 45-minute
焼がき食べ放題 **¥3,500**, or ¥4,000 with kaki rice and soup. November–March is the
*peak*, not the *season*. Also: it is the **別館 (annex)**, not "2F" — the 2F is the
ramen counter and the dining hall.
Source: https://www.sakana-ichiba.co.jp/eat/house/

### 2b. Kaki burger is ¥600, not ¥380
Official menu: 「松島かきバーガー **600円**」. The ¥380 figure survives only on
tourism-portal listings. Same page: メカメンチカツバーガー ¥600, カキフライ ¥600,
牛タン丼 ¥1,800, 串物各種 ¥500〜.
Source: https://www.sakana-ichiba.co.jp/eat/menu/

### 2c. Maguro-don is ¥1,300–2,400, not ¥750–2,400
The official menu's **cheapest** bowl of any kind is ねぎとろ丼 ¥1,300. Tuna bowls run
to 昭福丸まぐろ大漁丼 ¥2,400 (数量限定) and 昭福丸まぐろスペシャル丼 ¥2,400; the
seafood ceiling is 極上海鮮丼 ¥3,900 and 生うに丼 ¥3,950. Nothing at ¥750.

## 8. Seasonality, stated plainly for early-to-mid September

- **No autumn colour anywhere.** Naruko-kyo gorge peaks **late October**; Entsuin's
  maples likewise turn from late October. Aoba-dori is **summer green**, fully leafed.
- **Oysters are the day's central seasonal awkwardness.** Miyagi's headline
  shell-on/raw oyster season is a winter one, and the lunch billed as "Oyster Set
  Cuisine" will be **cooked** oyster. That is fine, and it is what every September
  oyster item in the batch leans on: the kaki burger (breaded, fried), Pensee's curry
  bread (fried), Kouha's fried and smoked sets, and — contrary to the worksheet —
  the Yakigaki House's year-round grill. What is genuinely gone is **raw**: the sea-urchin
  bowls closed at the end of August and the 松島かき丼 is season-limited.
- **Edamame (and therefore zunda) is at its peak** — the one thing that is better now
  than in winter.
- **Peak typhoon month.** The single largest schedule risk on Day 2 is the cruise being
  cancelled for weather, not any venue's hours.
- **Naruko will be quiet with the banners still up** if the worksheet's 5–6 September
  festival dates are right — see §6.9, unverified.
